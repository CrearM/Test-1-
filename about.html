Our Journey Begins on the Wrist

Founded in 2018, SmartWrist began with a simple yet ambitious idea: wearable technology shouldn't force you to compromise on personal style.

Our founders, a unique mix of horologists and software engineers, noticed a glaring gap in the market. Watches were either beautifully crafted but mechanically dated, or highly functional but aesthetically uninspiring hardware blocks.

We set out to create hybrid devices that look like premium fashion accessories while quietly tracking life-saving health metrics and keeping you connected. Today, SmartWrist is trusted by over a million users globally to elevate their daily routines.

OUR MISSION

To design intelligent wearables that empower individuals to live healthier, more connected lives without sacrificing elegance.

Health First

Unobtrusive tracking of heart rate, sleep quality, and active metrics to keep wellness at the forefront.

Sustainability

Using recycled aerospace-grade aluminum and vegan leather to ensure our footprint remains small.

Timeless Design

Curating luxury aesthetics so your smartwatch pairs with a tailored suit just as well as gym attire.

The Brains Behind the Beauty

Our proprietary WristOS isn't just another operating system; it's a completely optimized ecosystem designed to consume less power while delivering more data.

  • 14-Day Battery Life on a single 45-minute charge
  • Optical and Electrical Heart Sensors for ECG capabilities
  • Always-on Retina LTPO OLED display with variable refresh
  • Water resistance down to 50 meters (5 ATM rating)
